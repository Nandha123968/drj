OUR VISION & PHILOSOPHY

Movement, Recovery & Sustainable Wellness

The ultimate focus is not simply on treating pain or impairment, but on helping every individual achieve better movement, improved physical function, greater confidence, independence and lifelong physical wellbeing.

Our Core Vision

MANI Physiotherapy Clinic and Rehabilitation Centre is designed as a complete movement, rehabilitation and wellness destination, serving individuals from young adults to older adults and supporting women through pregnancy, post-natal recovery, and every stage of life.

By combining clinical physiotherapy, specialized rehabilitation, therapeutic exercise, fitness, ergonomics, women's wellness, preventive care, and patient education in one integrated setting, we provide personalized pathways from pain relief to peak function.

OUR CLINICAL MODEL

ABOUT THE CLINICIAN

Experienced care, centered around you.

“

Helping individuals regain mobility, improve function, prevent injury, and lead healthier lives through personalized rehabilitation, education and preventive healthcare.

Dr. C. Jayachitra Manikandan, P.T. is a highly experienced Senior Consultant Physiotherapist, Rehabilitation Specialist, and Certified Workplace Ergonomist with over 20 years of clinical and academic expertise.

Since beginning her professional career in 2004, she has focused on evidence-based rehabilitation for orthopaedic and neurological conditions, workplace ergonomics, postural correction, injury prevention, women's physiotherapy, and home-based rehabilitation.

She also serves as an Assistant Professor at KCP, Trichy, mentoring future physiotherapists and championing evidence-based practice. She has conducted extensive ergonomic training and health awareness programs across educational institutions, hospitals, and corporate organizations.
